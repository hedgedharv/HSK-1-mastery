🎯 Real HSK 1 Exam Simulation

Built from the published HSK 1 paper structure: 40 questions — 20 Listening + 20 Reading, four parts of five questions each. Each question is worth 5 points; 120/200 is the published pass mark.

Exam conditions
  • 40 questions · 200 points
  • Listening: 20 questions. Each audio item is played up to twice.
  • Reading: 20 questions. Pinyin is shown, as on the classic HSK 1 paper.
  • No instant answers or corrections during the exam.
  • When you finish, you get a detailed diagnostic by section and question type.

Important: this is an original simulation modelled on the published HSK 1 format, not a copied past paper. It is a readiness check, not an official result.

🆕 HSK 3.0 Level 1 Readiness Test

A separate HSK 3.0-style Level 1 simulation. It keeps the published 20 Listening + 20 Reading structure, but uses the revised question styles from the new sample materials. No writing section at Level 1.

Exam conditions
  • 40 questions · 200 points
  • 20 Listening + 20 Reading
  • 40-minute timer
  • Listening audio is limited to two plays per question
  • No pinyin is shown during Listening
  • No corrections are revealed until submission

What makes this different? HSK 3.0 Level 1 expands the Level 1 syllabus and changes some task formats. For example, the revised Listening includes question→answer selection, and Reading replaces the old picture true/false format with comprehension-style questions.

Important: this is an original readiness simulation based on the official HSK 3.0 structure/sample descriptions, not an official paper and not a guarantee of an official result. CTI describes the new HSK as covering tasks, topics, vocabulary, grammar and Chinese characters.
